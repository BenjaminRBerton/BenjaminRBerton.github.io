How to explore

  1. Switch between support, autonomous slow, and autonomous fast.
  2. Play or seek the paired cockpit and TARS-interface recordings; the red playhead follows them through the timeline.
  3. Use the side tabs to inspect pilot actions, gaze, aircraft control, flight state, and agent actions.

This privacy-safe demonstration was recorded by the researcher acting as the participant. It contains no research-participant data and is illustrative rather than part of the reported sample. The explorer is best viewed on a laptop or larger screen.
