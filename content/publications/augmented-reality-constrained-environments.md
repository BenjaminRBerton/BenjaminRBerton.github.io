This conference paper grew from work conducted through ESTIA's PEPSS platform in association with the Airbus/CORAC TOUCANS project. It addresses the difficulty of evaluating head-mounted augmented-reality systems in environments where physical constraints, display properties, task demands, and physiological effects interact.

The proposed methodology brings together physical, physiological, cognitive, and subjective measures to support a more faithful assessment of augmented-reality use in safety-critical and constrained settings.
