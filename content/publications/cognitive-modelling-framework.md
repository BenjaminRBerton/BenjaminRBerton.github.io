This paper presents a modular simulation framework for studying human–autonomy teaming in the flight deck. The system combines a QN-ACTR pilot model with SEEV-based visual attention, a finite-state-machine autonomous teammate, a shared human–autonomy interface, and an X-Plane simulation environment.

The framework produces complementary team-, task-, interaction-, and cognition-level measures. Its purpose is to support systematic comparison of task-allocation and interaction designs before committing to costly studies with professional pilots.

The paper demonstrates end-to-end execution across design conditions and describes how model-based and human-in-the-loop simulations can use the same system components.
