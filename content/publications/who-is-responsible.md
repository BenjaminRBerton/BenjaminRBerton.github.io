This work examines how the role of the commercial pilot may change in future single-pilot operations supported by increasingly capable onboard systems. It considers responsibilities, human–automation interaction, and the design implications of reorganizing work that is currently distributed between two pilots.

The work was presented at the 7th IFIP WG 13.6 and WG 13.5 Joint Working Conference on Human Work Interaction Design in Milan in 2024 and subsequently published in the conference proceedings.
