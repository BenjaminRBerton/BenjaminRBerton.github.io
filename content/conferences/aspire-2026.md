This demonstration presents Exoplanet Explorer as an open-source environment for teaching human–autonomy teaming. Attendees can coordinate autonomous rovers and drones, examine how changes in autonomy and available information affect collaboration, and identify requirements for observability, predictability, and directability.

The demonstration connects interactive gameplay to Interdependence Analysis. Rather than reasoning only from a written case, learners can test assumptions in the environment and revise task allocations or interface requirements based on observed system behaviour.

The session is scheduled for October 21, 2026, as part of the Cognitive Engineering & Human-AI Demonstrations program.
