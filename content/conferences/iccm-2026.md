This poster presents a cognitive model of a pilot interacting with an autonomous agent during takeoff. Implemented in QN-ACTR, the model combines procedural task knowledge with SEEV-based visual attention and mechanisms for verification, trust calibration, and reactive reliance.

Simulation conditions manipulate autonomy reliability and the timing of support. The model predicts how reliable automation can gradually reduce verification, how detected failures increase monitoring, and when delayed support can still be integrated before a task is completed.

The work was presented at ICCM 2026, co-located with the Annual Meeting of the Society for Mathematical Psychology in Montréal.
